Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shelf life, refrigeration, and ingredients.

What is the shelf life of Amul Kool Rose Milk? expand_more

Amul Kool Rose has a sealed shelf life of 180 days (6 months) from the date of manufacture. Because of our advanced ultra-high temperature sterilization (UHT) process and airtight sealing, it does not require refrigeration until opened. Once opened, consume immediately or refrigerate and consume within 24 hours.

Does Amul Kool contain any artificial chemical preservatives? expand_more

No. Amul Kool contains zero chemical preservatives. The long room-temperature shelf life is achieved strictly through thermal sterilization technology that eliminates bacteria while preserving the rich taste, bioavailable calcium, and dairy proteins.

Is the plastic bottle fully recyclable? expand_more

Yes, our 200ml bottles are manufactured from 100% recyclable food-grade PET plastic. Please rinse after drinking and discard in your designated plastic recycling bin.

Can I order bulk crates for weddings, cafes, or school canteens? expand_more

Yes! We provide dedicated institutional supply and customized event pricing for orders exceeding 10 crates (240 bottles). Please reach out to your nearest GCMMF regional branch office or submit an inquiry through our retail portal.
